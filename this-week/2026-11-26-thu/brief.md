### 1. Manitoba bans smart watches and AI glasses in schools · score 9/10
Manitoba's education minister sent a Nov. 17 letter to all superintendents (and publicly funded independent schools) adding smart glasses, smart watches and other wearables to the school cellphone ban, in place since September 2024. It covers devices with "messaging, social media, internet browsing, photography, audio or video recording, artificial intelligence tools, or other cellphone-like functions." It came a week after Hanover School Division banned smart glasses, telling parents they "can record audio and video without others readily knowing." Minister Tracy Schmidt: "As technology evolves, so must our guidelines to ensure students are learning in healthy, safe environments." No exceptions are described; MediaSmarts' Kara Brisson-Boivin noted students with visual or hearing impairments "could be aided" by the technology. · [CBC News, Nov. 24](https://ca.news.yahoo.com/smart-watches-glasses-no-longer-183033392.html) · [CBC (Cina Radio copy)](https://www.cinaradio.com/2026/11/24/smart-watches-and-glasses-no-longer-allowed-in-manitoba-schools/)
**Why it matters:** Most school phone rules name phones. Wearables with cameras and AI are already here, so a rule written for the last device ages fast. And a ban with no accessibility exception will hit the students the tech helps most.
