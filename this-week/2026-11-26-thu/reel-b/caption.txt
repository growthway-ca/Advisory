Manitoba just added smart watches, smart glasses and other wearables to its school cellphone ban.

The education minister's Nov. 17 letter to superintendents covers devices with "messaging, social media, internet browsing, photography, audio or video recording, artificial intelligence tools, or other cellphone-like functions." The phone ban itself has been in place since September 2024. Hanover School Division banned smart glasses a week earlier, warning parents they "can record audio and video without others readily knowing."

Minister Tracy Schmidt: "As technology evolves, so must our guidelines to ensure students are learning in healthy, safe environments."
