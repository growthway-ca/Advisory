This week in AI & schools 🇨🇦

1. Google.org is funding Experience AI training for 7,000 Canadian educators, aiming to reach an estimated 300,000 young people by 2028.
2. Manitoba added smart watches, smart glasses and other wearables, including ones with AI tools, to its school cellphone ban.
3. A Brock University survey of 441 Ontario secondary teachers: 90.7% know of students using generative AI for schoolwork, but only 15.9% rate their own skills as advanced or expert. 56% strongly agree teachers need time to work it in.
4. P.E.I.'s education minister says a "bell-to-bell" phone ban "is indeed coming."

Next up: Hour of AI Week, Dec. 7–11.

The pattern: rules for devices are moving fast. Time and training for teachers aren't keeping up. Which one would you fix first? 👇

Sources: Raspberry Pi Foundation (Nov. 20); CBC News (Nov. 24 and 26); The Conversation, Sunaina Sharma, Brock University (Nov. 26).
