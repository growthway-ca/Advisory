### 1. Ontario teachers: students are ahead on AI, and only 15.9% of teachers rate themselves advanced · score 10/10
Writing in The Conversation (Nov. 26), Brock University's Sunaina Sharma reports a 2025 survey of 441 Ontario secondary teachers, supported by Brock and OSSTF: "90.7 per cent of teachers reported direct knowledge of students using GenAI for academic work." "Only 15.9 per cent of surveyed teachers rated their own GenAI proficiency as advanced or expert." "More than half of respondents (56 per cent) strongly agreed that teachers need time to integrate GenAI, while 43 per cent strongly agreed training is necessary." Teachers split: "I think trying to ban the use of GenAI in schools is pointless — it is here to stay" vs. "AI is NOT needed in education." · [The Conversation via Yahoo](https://ca.news.yahoo.com/ontario-high-school-teachers-speak-160332692.html) · [the study (Brock)](https://brocku.scholaris.ca/items/ade47ad6-45e9-4406-b04b-b4d890188f49)
**Why it matters:** It's Growthway's core point in Ontario teachers' own numbers: students moved first, teachers want time and training before anything else.
