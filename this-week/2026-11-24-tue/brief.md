### 1. Free AI training for 7,000 Canadian educators by 2028 · score 8/10
The Raspberry Pi Foundation (Nov. 20) will "expand Experience AI across Canada to train 7,000 educators who will reach an estimated 300,000 young people across the country by 2028," with $2 million from Google.org, working with Actua and Digital Moment. It describes Experience AI as "a free educational programme that helps teachers and students learn about artificial intelligence." A 2023 Canadian pilot with Digital Moment reached 500 educators and an estimated 56,000 young people. This is part of the Google.org Canada package covered yesterday. · [Raspberry Pi Foundation](https://www.raspberrypi.org/blog/bringing-ai-education-to-300000-young-people-across-canada/) · [Google Canada](https://blog.google/intl/en-ca/company-news/outreach-initiatives/empowering-canadian-youth-families-and-educators/)

### P1. Ontario's budget bill would pause a tax exemption non-profits say affordable housing depends on · score 9/10
Bill 143, the "Plan to Protect Ontario Act (Budget Measures), 2026 (No. 2)," amends the Assessment Act in a way critics say will suspend a property tax exemption for rental housing starting in 2027. Lawyers for 24 non-profit housing agencies (including United Way Elgin Middlesex, Indwell and CMHA Thames Valley) say property taxes can be "approximately one-third of an affordable housing provider's operating costs," and warn of "a stoppage in the development of affordable housing in Ontario." Properties already exempt keep it; projects in development would lose it. The Finance Ministry calls it a temporary pause for consultations and points to an optional municipal reduction of up to 35%. The bill has passed second reading and is at committee. · [CP24 / CTV London](https://cp24.com/politics/queens-park/2026/11/23/non-profits-fear-housing-halt-as-ontario-bill-targets-tax-exemption)
