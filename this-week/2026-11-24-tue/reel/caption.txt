Two Google stories this week, and both land in your school.

1. Google.org is funding over $7M CAD for Canadian youth safety and AI literacy non-profits. Part of it ($2M) lets the Raspberry Pi Foundation, Actua and Digital Moment train 7,000 Canadian educators in Experience AI, a free AI education program, aiming to reach an estimated 300,000 young people by 2028.
2. More than 540 Google employees and contractors signed a petition asking the company to let schools keep Google Search but turn off the AI answers (reported by Bloomberg).

Meanwhile, Google's AI tools go off by default for K-12 student accounts in early 2027, and keeping plain Search means accepting AI features.
