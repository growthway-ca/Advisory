### 1. CUPE education workers vote 94.2%, the fifth Ontario education union with a strike mandate · score 8/10
The Ontario School Board Council of Unions, representing "over 57,000 CUPE education workers across public, Catholic, English, and French school boards," released a 94.2% strike vote Thursday night. President Joe Tigani said members are "ready to take strike action if necessary." The union is asking for more staffing in every classification, safer schools, better working conditions and job security, and says members are "incredibly overworked." Central bargaining begins Nov. 30. CP24 calls it the "fifth major education union to vote in favour of possible job action." · [CP24](https://www.cp24.com/politics/queens-park/2026/11/20/cupe-ontario-education-workers-vote-in-favour-of-strike-action-ahead-of-bargaining/)
**Why it matters:** These are the support staff schools run on, and all five education unions now hold mandates. For parents, the message stays calm: a mandate isn't a strike, and Nov. 30 is the date to watch.
