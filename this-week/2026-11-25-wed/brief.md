### 1. Hour of AI Week, Dec. 7–11: free AI literacy resources for Canadian classrooms · score 7/10
Ampere and Canada Learning Code (Nov. 24) will run AI literacy activities during Hour of AI Week, Dec. 7–11, part of Computer Science Education Week. Hour of AI "is a global initiative led by CodeAI." They're offering "free classroom resources" (CodeAI activities and lesson plans, Ampere's AI Glossary), virtual AI workshops for educators and adult learners including an Indigenous perspective on AI, and in-person sessions at makerspaces. Details at amp.ca/teachers/hour-of-ai. · [Release](https://www.newswire.ca/news-releases/ampere-and-canada-learning-code-bring-national-ai-literacy-initiative-to-classrooms-across-canada-853821017.html)

### P1. Bill 143 now hits student housing too: universities warn of tax on up to 12,000 student homes · score 8/10
New since yesterday: the Council of Ontario Universities' Steve Orsini told a Queen's Park committee that up to 12,000 existing student homes would have to pay property tax if Bill 143 passes, and another 10,000 planned student homes could be put in jeopardy. Global News reports the bill would end the exemption for some providers, "including charitable providers and student residences," in 2027. NDP finance critic Jessica Bell: "It doesn't make any sense to me." A lawyer for Indigenous housing providers says it's coming "without any meaningful consultation." The government calls it a "temporary pause" after a June 2025 Court of Appeal ruling and says existing exemptions stay. · [Global News](https://globalnews.ca/news/12164088/ontario-property-tax-change-could-hike-affordable-housing-costs-critics/) · [CP24 (yesterday)](https://cp24.com/politics/queens-park/2026/11/23/non-profits-fear-housing-halt-as-ontario-bill-targets-tax-exemption)
