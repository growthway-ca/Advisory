Ties to Matt's position: free post-secondary, and reinvent how it operates.

## Sources
- Council of Ontario Universities, *Backgrounder: One-Year Anniversary of the Blue-Ribbon Panel Report* (Nov. 2024): Ontario universities are the lowest funded per student in the country; provincial operating grants per domestic FTE, 2021–22: Canada excluding Ontario $15,428, Ontario $8,788. The last per-student funding increase was in 2008–09; "the 10% cut and freeze to tuition". https://ontariosuniversities.ca/docs/backgrounder-one-year-anniversary-of-brp-report
- Colleges Ontario, 2026 Ontario budget submission (Dec. 2025): colleges have cut $1.4 billion in costs, "suspending more than 600 programs and eliminating over 8,000 staff positions in the past year"; projected deficit of up to $1.5 billion by 2027–28. https://cdn.agilitycms.com/colleges-ontario/website/modules/CO_OntarioBudgetSubmission_2026_EN_Final.pdf
