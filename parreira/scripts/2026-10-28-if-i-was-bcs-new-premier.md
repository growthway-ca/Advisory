## Sources
- Elections BC, preliminary results (Oct. 25, 2026, 17:04 PST): Conservatives leading in 63 districts with 48.05% of the vote; preliminary turnout 48.12%. https://results.elections.bc.ca/
- BC Conservative platform, *Lorne Doerkson's Plan for a Stronger BC* (2026): a $1,000 income-tax cut per person by raising the basic personal amount to $32,000, est. $2.9 billion a year; "Help families when it counts" $150 million (full annual cost); ends the "safe supply" approach; mandatory care. https://conservativebc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/20261016_BCC_Platform2026_8-5x11-Draft-18_compressed.pdf
- CCPA, *The $10-a-day divide: Child care fees in Canada in 2026* (Aug. 2026): B.C. infant fees from $28 (Kelowna) to $52 (Richmond), the highest in Canada; not-for-profits cheaper than for-profits "at every point in the fee distribution in every city studied"; about 12,400 B.C. spaces at $10/day, 12% of those space types. https://www.policyalternatives.ca/news-research/the-10-a-day-divide-child-care-fees-in-canada-in-2026/

The "target relief, put the difference into child care" move is Matt's proposal. Don't put a cost on it on screen unless we find one.
