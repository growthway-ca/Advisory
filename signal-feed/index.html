    How it works

    By the time it’s out for tender, the spec is written.

    Stage 1 of 6

    Application filed

    A rezoning or site plan application lands at the municipality. From this day the project is public record.

    Stage 2 of 6

    Council decision

    Public meeting, staff report, vote. Size and use are settled in the open.

    Stage 3 of 6

    Site plan approved

    Layout, floor area and servicing are fixed. The design team starts choosing products.

    Stage 4 of 6

    Building permit

    Drawings and specifications are complete.

    Stage 5 of 6

    Tender

    Trades and suppliers are asked to price what has already been specified.

    Stage 6 of 6

    Construction

    Orders go to whoever won.

      Questions

      What suppliers ask first.

      Isn’t all of this public already?

      Yes. Every item comes from public council and planning records. It is spread across 18 municipal websites, inside agenda packages that can run to hundreds of pages. We read all of it each week so your sales team doesn’t have to.

      How is this different from a tender service?

      A tender service tells you when a job is out for bids. By then the products are specified. Signal Feed tells you when the project is first filed, so you can talk to the owner, architect and engineer while those choices are still open.

      How do you know what a project will need?

      From what is on file: building type, floor area, storeys, units and servicing. The product tags are our read of that, not a spec. The source document is linked so you can check.

      We sell outside Simcoe County. Can you cover our region?

      Not yet. Reply to the sample issue with where you sell, and we’ll let you know when that region opens.
