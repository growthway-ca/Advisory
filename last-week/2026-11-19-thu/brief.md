### 1. Ontario elementary teachers vote 97%: three teacher unions, three strike mandates in three days · score 9/10
ETFO (about 84,000 members) says 97% of its teachers and occasional teachers and 97% of its education workers voted for central strike action. President David Mastin: members are "frustrated by years of chronic underfunding" and "deteriorating learning and working conditions"; the union names smaller class sizes, classroom complexity, health and safety, job security and fair compensation. He also said Minister Calandra's suggestion that the votes simply reflect a desire for fair deals "misses the point entirely." ETFO says bargaining resumes at both central tables at the end of November. AEFO and CUPE-OSBCU results are still to come. · [ETFO release](https://www.etfo.ca/news-publications/media-releases/97-per-cent-vote-yes) · [CBC via Yahoo](https://ca.news.yahoo.com/ontario-english-public-elementary-teachers-232421587.html)

### 2. Google's AI-off default for K-12 starts in early 2027; researchers call for joint standards · score 8/10
In The Conversation (Nov. 17), researchers from The Dais at Toronto Metropolitan University and Western University write that Google's AI tools, "including Gemini and Gemini Notebook, would be turned off by default for kindergarten to Grade 12 student accounts starting in early 2027," and that schools wanting plain Google search "will have to accept Google's AI." They argue governments, boards, unions and parents "could set common regulatory standards for AI products sold to Canadian schools," and that safety questions "shouldn't be settled by school boards alone." They also note Ontario made "Cyber Safety and Artificial Intelligence" one of four mandatory PA-day topics this year. · [The Conversation](https://theconversation.com/googles-ai-ultimatum-exposes-the-risks-of-schools-dependence-on-big-tech-296467) · [Google's announcement](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/education/ai-learning-era-updates-2026/)
