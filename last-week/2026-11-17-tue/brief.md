### 1. Ontario Catholic teachers vote 98% for a strike mandate · score 8/10
OECTA, which represents 45,000 English Catholic teachers, says 98% of members voted Nov. 12–13 to authorize strike action "if necessary," citing "overcrowded classrooms, staffing shortages, escalating violence, and dwindling resources and supports." OECTA says it "is not in a legal strike position at this time." Education Minister Paul Calandra said he's "actually encouraged by a vote that is that high because it shows that teachers want us at the table," but wants to wait for the Ontario Labour Relations Board's decision on what gets bargained centrally. OSSTF's vote closed Nov. 16; AEFO, ETFO and CUPE-OSBCU results are due after Nov. 17–18. · [OECTA release](https://www.manilatimes.net/2026/11/16/tmt-newswire/globenewswire/98-vote-yes-catholic-teachers-deliver-resounding-strike-mandate-demand-action-at-bargaining-table/2471214) · [The Canadian Press](https://ca.news.yahoo.com/ontario-catholic-teachers-vote-overwhelmingly-171502899.html) · [Ottawa Citizen](https://ca.news.yahoo.com/ontarios-english-catholic-teachers-union-155357382.html)
**Why it matters:** Parents will see "98%" and worry. The useful, neutral message: a mandate isn't a strike, and nothing can happen until the Labour Board rules and the legal steps play out.

## Politics (Matt · Bluesky only)

### P1. Poverty council: 4.5 million Canadians still in poverty; it recommends an income floor · score 7/10
The National Advisory Council on Poverty's 2026 report, "Too many left behind," says "In 2024, 4.5 million people, 11.0% of Canadians, continued to live in poverty." That's a 26% drop from 2015 but "short of the legislated target of a 50% reduction by 2030." Among its recommendations: regional income floors so everyone reaches "at least Canada's Official Poverty Line," and doubling non-market housing "from 3.5% to at least 7.1%." For contrast, IRPP's read of Budget 2026: the Groceries and Essentials Benefit increase lifts 120,000 people out of poverty in 2027 "but it is a temporary top-up." · [NACP 2026 report](https://www.canada.ca/en/employment-social-development/programs/poverty-reduction/national-advisory-council/reports/2026-annual.html) · [Policy Options (IRPP)](https://policyoptions.irpp.org/2026/11/budget-2026-poverty-homelessness/)
