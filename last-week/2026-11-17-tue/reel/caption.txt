Alberta is changing what every high school student needs to graduate.

Starting with Grade 10 in fall 2028: 47 core credits instead of 56, up to 53 optional credits instead of 44, still 100 in total. New required credits in physical education, wellness and financial literacy, and at least one diploma exam. All senior grades follow by 2030.

The Alberta Teachers' Association says flexibility "should expand students' futures, not weaken the foundation they need."
