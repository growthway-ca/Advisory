### 1. Ontario high school teachers vote 96% for a strike mandate; bargaining starts next week · score 8/10
OSSTF announced Nov. 17 that 96% of its teachers and 92% of its education workers (education assistants, library workers, custodians, school safety monitors) voted for a strike mandate. President Martha Hradowy pointed to "ballooning class sizes," staffing shortages and cuts to supports such as speech-language pathologists and social workers: "We should not be going to the bargaining table, just asking for the basics in public education." Education Minister Paul Calandra: "These numbers aren't surprising or shocking to me," adding "I have to balance taxpayers." Both reports say central negotiations begin next week; neither reports a Labour Relations Board ruling yet. ETFO, AEFO and CUPE-OSBCU results are still to come. · [The Canadian Press](https://ca.news.yahoo.com/ontario-secondary-teachers-vote-96-163007457.html) · [Global News](https://globalnews.ca/news/12154581/ontario-secondary-school-teachers-federation-strike-vote/)
**Why it matters:** Two unions, two near-unanimous mandates in two days. For parents, the useful message hasn't changed (a mandate is not a strike), but the new news is that talks restart next week. Growthway stays out of the bargaining.
