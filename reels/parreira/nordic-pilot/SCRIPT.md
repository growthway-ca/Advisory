# Pilot: "If I ran a Nordic spa"

Parreira Advisory's first on-camera reel, in the format of the reference reels: Matt on camera over full-bleed
photos of an invented brand, word-by-word captions, a serif hook, and a comment call to action at the end.
It retells The Nordic Fix with an invented spa, **Lumi Bath House**, so no real business is shown or named.

Target length: about 45 seconds.

## The brand: Lumi Bath House

- **Name:** Lumi ("snow" in Finnish). Full name Lumi Bath House, "Lumi" on its own for small marks.
- **Feel:** quiet, warm, Scandinavian. Wood, steam, snow at the windows, dusk light.
- **Palette:** spruce `#1F3A33`, steam white `#F4F1EA`, cedar amber `#C08A4E`, slate `#3B4246`.
- **Logo:** lowercase "lumi" in a soft, rounded serif, with a single thin curl of steam rising from the dot of the i.
  It goes on the bracelet, the board, the bistro tables, robes, towels and the door.

## The script

Read it the way you'd tell a friend. The `/` marks are breaths; leave a clear beat at each line break so the edit
has room to cut.

> **[HOOK, full frame]**
> If I ran a Nordic spa today / here's how I'd fix it.
>
> Last month I spent five hundred dollars at one. / The smartest thing in the building / was the bracelet on my wrist.
> And all it did / was open a locker.
>
> In the hot stone room, / the sand timers were broken. / The door opened every few minutes, / people checking for a free bay.
> At the bistro, / runners were calling out food / nobody could find.
>
> So here's Lumi. / Same bracelet, / doing real work.
> A board outside the stone room. / Green is free, / red is taken. / Nobody opens the door.
> Tap in at your bay, / and it buzzes you at fifteen minutes.
> Tap your table, / and lunch finds you.
> And for first-timers, / it guides you around the circuit.
>
> **[CTA, full frame]**
> The fix was already on every wrist. / Comment "FIX" / and I'll send you the playbook.

About 130 words, which comes to roughly 45–50 seconds at a natural pace.

## Recording it

- **Phone vertical**, at eye level, about an arm's length plus a bit away. Frame from mid-chest up, with a little
  space above your head.
- **Light:** face a window, never stand with it behind you. Daytime, no overhead lights if you can help it.
- **Background:** a plain wall, ideally a light, even colour. I'll cut you out, and plain walls cut cleanly.
- **Wear** a solid, darker top (navy, charcoal, forest). Avoid white, green and fine stripes.
- **Sound:** a quiet room with a rug or soft furniture. A clip-on mic is a big step up if you have one; otherwise
  hold the phone closer rather than raising your voice.
- **Look into the lens**, not at the screen. Do 2–3 full takes and send the one that feels most like you.
  Stumbles are fine: just pause and repeat the line, and I'll cut around it.
- **Send** the original file (AirDrop to your computer, or from Photos or Drive), not a screen recording or a
  compressed chat version.
