# Parreira.ca episode ideas

Matt's personal video series in the "If I was…" format: he sits under a screen, and the screen builds the story with
stickers, logos, labels, counters and progress bars while he talks. Every episode follows the same shape:

1. **Cold open (2 s):** a board of faces or businesses. Matt taps one. The name slams in.
2. **The tension:** "Despite…", with one or two real numbers on screen, each with a small source tag.
3. **"Here's what I do":** First → Then → Finally, three concrete moves, each built up on the screen.
4. **Payoff + ask:** one line, then "Comment who (or what) I should take on next."

60–90 seconds, captions 2–3 words at a time, a cut or a new sticker every 1–2 seconds.

## Rules

- Every number on screen comes from a source we opened, and the screen shows a short source tag ("CFIB, Oct. 2026").
- Real logos and faces only as the subject of commentary ("before" logos, official portraits, press photos we're allowed
  to use), never as a fake endorsement or a fake official rebrand. Concept work is labelled "concept".
- Politics episodes argue about policies and records, not people (same rules as the Bluesky politics track).
- Business episodes are ideas, not financial advice; say so in the caption when an episode talks about buying a business.
- Parreira.ca only. Growthway stays non-partisan and never runs these.

## Series

### Politics: "If I was Minister of ___" / "If I was premier of ___"
- If I was B.C.'s new premier (scripted, see the `social` branch: `parreira/scripts/`)
- If I was Ontario's education minister: trustees back, one provincial AI policy, PD before rollout
- If I was Minister of Small Business: help employees buy their retiring boss's business (check current employee
  ownership trust rules before scripting)
- If I was Minister of Health: free training for doctors and nurses in exchange for service in short-staffed regions
- If I was finance minister for a day: where an across-the-board tax cut's money could go instead

### Business: "If I bought ___" (entrepreneurship through acquisition)
- If I bought a 30-year-old HVAC company (scripted)
- If I bought a laundromat / driving school / commercial cleaning company / print shop / tutoring centre
- Hook data to reuse: CFIB "Passing the Torch" (Oct. 2026): 63% of owners plan to exit within a decade, 6% have a
  formal succession plan

### Business: "If I rebranded ___"
- A credit union, a community college, a small-town tourism board, a provincial ministry website, a post office counter
- Old logo left, concept building on the right; label it "concept"

### Business: "If I launched ___ in a boring industry"
- A school-bus contractor that runs on software
- A snow-removal subscription
- Edtech contract review for school boards (hook: Microsoft's binding student AI standards cover US schools only)

### Business: "If I added one service to ___"
- A dental office: a membership plan for people without insurance
- A hardware store: an install service
- Growthway: AI policy-in-a-box for boards

### Business: "If I built an incubator in ___"
- Sudbury, Moncton, Kamloops: anchor to the local college and hospital, founders-in-residence for people buying
  retiring owners' businesses, a shared back office

### Post-secondary: "If I ran ___"
- If I ran an Ontario university (scripted)
- If I ran a college in a town losing international students
- If I ran a med school
- Matt's position: free post-secondary, and reinvent how it operates

### Engagement formats
- **Minister for a day (you pick):** viewers vote in the comments on next week's face
- **The receipt:** promise vs. record with stamped checkmarks (e.g. B.C. at 100 days)
- **Steal this policy:** a policy that works somewhere else, with flags on the board
- **US got it, we didn't:** a split screen
