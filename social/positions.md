# Positions

Stances Matt and Growthway have already taken in public. The daily brief keeps new drafts consistent with these, and
flags any story that would push against one. Add a line whenever a post takes a clear position.

## Matt (personal)

- **The adoption–instruction gap is the policy problem, not the technology.** Students are already using AI; the
  failure is that schools haven't caught up with guidance and teaching. (Bluesky, KPMG survey post)
- **Parents aren't asking "how do I catch my kid?"** They're asking whether they're equipped to have a useful
  conversation with their kid about AI. (Bluesky)

## Growthway (organization)

- Practical, school-ready AI literacy over bans or hype.
- Parents are part of the solution: parent nights, plain-language guidance.
- Teachers need PD and clear policy before they're asked to enforce anything.
