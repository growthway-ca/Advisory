# Positions

Stances Matt and Growthway have already taken in public. The daily brief keeps new drafts consistent with these, and
flags any story that would push against one. Add a line whenever a post takes a clear position.

## Matt (personal)

- **The adoption–instruction gap is the policy problem, not the technology.** Students are already using AI; the
  failure is that schools haven't caught up with guidance and teaching. (Bluesky, KPMG survey post)
- **Parents aren't asking "how do I catch my kid?"** They're asking whether they're equipped to have a useful
  conversation with their kid about AI. (Bluesky)

## Growthway (organization)

- Practical, school-ready AI literacy over bans or hype.
- Parents are part of the solution: parent nights, plain-language guidance.
- Teachers need PD and clear policy before they're asked to enforce anything.

## Matt (politics, Bluesky)

Matt is openly progressive. He gave these views to the brief on Oct. 26, 2026; they aren't all public posts yet. Once one
is posted, note the post here like the education positions above.

- **Train doctors and nurses for free:** cover medical and nursing school (paid training) to fix the health workforce shortage.
- **Universal basic income.**
- **Free post-secondary, and reinvent how it operates.** Cost isn't the only problem; how institutions are run and funded needs rethinking too.
- **Free, universal child care.**
- **Tax the rich:** a fairer system where the highest incomes and wealth pay their share; skeptical of across-the-board tax cuts that mostly help those who need it least.
- **Less corporate influence in government:** less corporate sponsorship, lobbying and money in politics.
- **Help people who are homeless with what they need:** addiction care, food and housing, treated as health and dignity, not just enforcement.

Added by the brief as fitting the above (Matt to confirm or strike):

- Non-market and social housing, and stronger renter protections.
- Affordable, reliable public transit.
- Workers' rights and a living wage.
- Climate action that's fair to working people.
